02 / THE QUICK TAKE

Two moods.
One screen.

01 / OPEN●

Android

Playful, flexible and built for tinkerers.

Under the hood: Linux kernel → Android Runtime → your apps.

✿ A flexible, Linux-based system running apps on a virtual translation layer (ART), allowing it to work on any phone hardware but requiring more RAM. ♡
02 / FOCUSED◒

iOS

Polished, private and designed as one neat ecosystem.

Under the hood: Darwin core → Cocoa Touch → your apps.

✿ A closed, Unix-based system running apps directly on custom chips, delivering highly efficient performance with less RAM. ♡
